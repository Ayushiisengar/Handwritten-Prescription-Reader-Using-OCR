About MediLexor

MediLexor is an AI-powered prescription reader designed to interpret handwritten medical prescriptions and detect medicine names along with their generic equivalents. By combining OCR and machine learning, the system helps patients and healthcare professionals understand prescriptions quickly and accurately.
